What draft reads, and when

draft reads a page only when you ask it to — a press of its icon, a fill, or the automatic reading you switch on — and sends what it reads only to draft's own server (jobsviadraft.com). Nothing is sold, and no AI reads your pages.

  • Nothing is sold.
  • No AI reads your pages.
  • When you press the toolbar iconThe page's address and the job posting's text go to draft, to find the job and your match. Signed out, only the employer's name and the page's address go — to show that employer's public record — and nothing is stored.
  • When you fill an applicationYour saved answers and résumé come from draft and are typed into the form on that page. draft never submits a form on its own — you press Submit.
  • When you turn on automatic readingOnly where you switch it on in Settings — the application sites you choose, or job postings on any site: those pages are read as they open, so the job and its form are ready. Turn it off there at any time.
  • When you use the runnerAfter you click Fill all, it opens your waiting applications in background tabs and fills them. Only your press of Send all sends them, from your own browser.
  • For the toolbar badgeWhen a page finishes loading — at most every five minutes — draft asks its server for today's count of new matches. Nothing about the page is sent.
  • In your browserYour sign-in and your settings — the privacy policy lists each one.
