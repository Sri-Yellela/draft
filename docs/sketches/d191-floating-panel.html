What Chrome allows, and where that bends the 11-14 row

Opening by itself needs page access on the boards

Chrome lets an extension draw on a page before you click only on sites it has access to. Today the board sites are optional and come from one prompt, the "read and prepare" consent you already give in Options (D97 T12). D191 (6) said "activeTab only", and the panel cannot open by itself under that rule.

Recommended: the panel opens by itself only on boards you have allowed. Until then, the first press of the toolbar icon opens it and offers "Open on job boards by itself", which shows one Chrome prompt for all eight board families. No install warning, and nothing is added for other sites.

Under 300 ms without keeping your data

D191 (4) says the extension keeps nothing of yours on the computer beyond the session token. So the first frame is drawn from the page itself: the title, company and place from the page's own JSON-LD or headings, with no network call. That takes about 100 ms after the page is ready. draft's facts (fit, stack, sponsorship, run state) fill in when the answer arrives, typically 300–700 ms. Choose Arriving above to see it.

Recommended: also keep the last answer per posting in Chrome's session storage, cleared when Chrome closes, so a reopen or back-navigation is instant.

Pages the panel cannot reach

Without access to every site (D191 (6) forbids it), the /d square cannot sit on every page. On an unrecognised page the toolbar icon is the way in, and the press draws the panel's general view on that page. Chrome draws nothing on its own pages (chrome://, the Web Store, the PDF viewer). There the icon opens jobsviadraft.com/app.

Never over the employer's Apply button

Before drawing, the panel finds the page's apply control (the button or link that says "Apply"). If its box meets the panel's corner, the panel moves to bottom-left, then top-right. Choose Apply button bottom-right above to see it. A spot you drag the panel to is remembered per site and always wins.

What you decide

  1. The layout: A corner card, B edge sheet, or C one-line strip. Recommended: A. It is closest to what you pointed at, and the dock's frame already does everything it needs.
  2. Opening by itself: only after the one board prompt (recommended), or ask for board access at install. Install-time access adds a Chrome warning and changes D191 (6).
  3. The fit line: the word draft already shows (Good fit / Stretch / Long shot from the board's cut points), or a percentage as the 11-14 row draws it ("86% match"). Recommended: the word. The board shows the word too.
  4. "Review before I submit": the row puts it in the collapsed panel. In draft every application waits for you unless Apply for me is on, so the box would really be the Apply-for-me switch for this job. Keep it, or show the state as plain words ("Waits for your approval")?
