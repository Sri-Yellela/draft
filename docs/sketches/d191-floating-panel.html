What changes

  • One panel. The dock and Chrome's side panel both go. Fill, your day, search and Ask draft all live in this one panel.
  • Opens by itself on job boards (your pick): the job boards are asked for at install. Chrome will show "Read and change your data on greenhouse.io, lever.co…" when it installs. This replaces the old "activeTab only" rule (D191 (6)). You can switch it off at the bottom of the panel.
  • Instant: the job's name and company come from the page itself, so the panel shows before draft answers. The match percentage and sponsorship fill in a moment later.
  • Review before I submit (your pick) is a real switch: on, the application waits for you; off, Apply for me sends it for this job.
  • Never over the employer's Apply button: the panel moves to the other side. Try "Right" above.
  • Closed is the /d square, a rounded square, not a capsule. It pulses once on a new match.
