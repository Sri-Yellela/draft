03 LEG 3 · WHAT SEPARATES SENIORS · ≈ 2 MINHazards on the ridge

Receiving the POST is easy ground. These three are where production systems slip, and they’re what the interviewer is waiting to hear.

HAZARD ① · TRUSTPIN ① Strangers at the doorAnyone can POST to your URL. The provider signs the body with a shared secret; recompute it and turn away any mismatch.
HAZARD ② · SPEEDPIN ② A slow reply looks deadAnswer 200 OK at once. Drop the event on a queue and do the heavy work in the background.
HAZARD ③ · REPEATSPIN ③ The same news, twiceIf you’re down, the provider retries with backoff, sometimes for days. Store each event ID and skip the repeats.
