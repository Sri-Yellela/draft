The deck is DeckView.jsx's: a fan of real cards, the front one face-up. Turn it with a sideways flick, the arrows, J / K or ↑ ↓; the arriving card flips face-up. Scroll down on the front card, press it or press Enter and it becomes the long post, in place; scroll up at its top or press Esc to fold it. Drag it right to draft, left to pass. Opening a posting from the list shows it the same way, on the deck.
