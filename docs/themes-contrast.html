/draft — two high-contrast themes

What changes: only the colour tokens. The neutrals — canvas, surfaces, text, borders — go to pure black and white (no cream, no warm charcoal). The brand stays: the logo orange, and green, red, amber and blue for indicators — each re-tuned so its text clears 7:1. Depth, glass, grain, radii, type and layout are untouched: the same --c-* names are re-pointed, exactly as colorTokens.js was designed for. Contrast Light and Contrast Dark are inverses on the neutrals. Shape cues (a glyph, an edge style, a pattern) are now an optional switch that works on every theme — turn it on above to see it. All values are samples to approve.
